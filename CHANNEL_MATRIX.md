## Changes from the audited interface

Student→Professor submit/10 becomes submit/11; Professor→University submit/8 becomes submit/9; University→Competency submit/7 becomes submit/8, each adding s. Ledger anchor_request/0 becomes anchor_request/1 carrying c. Ledger's old update_profile/2 receiver is replaced by the independent `c:anchor_confirm()` call on t_Anchor. Profile aggregation retains co/h update_profile/2; it no longer requires a ledger token after ledger completion.

New abort/1 receivers are selected by actual source phase and outcome guard. deny/1 carries the provided student signature through Wallet/Competency to a mismatch guard in CredentialObject. deny_issuer/1 carries the proposed issuer signature to its mismatch guard. ledger_failed/0 synchronizes failure from Ledger through Competency to CredentialObject and both user-facing peers. anchor_confirm/0 synchronizes Ledger→Competency→CredentialObject+Student+Wallet after commit.

Rows marked EXCLUDED are intentionally incompatible **receiver alternatives**, not a missing required endpoint: for example CANCELLED cannot use an awaiting-anchor receiver whose guard admits only LEDGER_REJECTED. Source markings restrict the available phase as well. Every intended phase is exercised in the fifteen directed scenarios. The negative tests verify bad-signature acceptance/rejection, invalid issuer cancellation, valid-signature denial and pre-anchor VP gating are disabled. No dynamic mismatch is hidden by the static signature table.
